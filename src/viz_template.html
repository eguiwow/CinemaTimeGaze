How each decade splits

The distribution behind the medians. Each bar is one release decade, showing only the films that travel — set in their own present is set aside and reported on the right, because at 60–80% of every decade it would otherwise crush the pattern. Blue looks back, red looks ahead, and the darker the shade the further it goes. Decades under 10 targets are omitted.

How this was built, and what is wrong with it
  • Corpus. __SOURCE_NOTE__
  • Labels are model judgments, not extractions. __KPCT__% of films were classified from world knowledge rather than from a stated year in the source text, because the source text is a ~90-word Wikipedia lead that usually does not name the setting. Those labels have not been checked against an independent ground truth. Treat the shape as a hypothesis, not a measurement.
  • Present is the default and the default is sticky. A film whose setting is never stated and that the model does not recognise lands on "present". That biases the whole picture toward the diagonal, so real nostalgia is probably understated.
  • Medians, never means. One film set in the year 802,701 outweighs a thousand set last Tuesday. Every figure here is a median; the means are meaningless and are not shown.
  • The year axis is piecewise. The dense 1850–2050 band gets most of the width; deep past and far future are compressed, with dashed breaks marking the joins. Distances across a break are not comparable.
  • Re-releases contaminate. A 2013 3-D re-release of a 1993 film reads as a 20-year lookback. Rare, but present.
